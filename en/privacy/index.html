    Nexora (the "Operator") processes personal information as described below to provide the Ollarai beta service (the "Service").

    1. Information We Collect

    • When you sign up: email address and password (encrypted and stored by our authentication provider; the Operator cannot see the original password)
    • When you use the Service: questions you enter, query history generated from those questions, daily usage count, last access time, and IP address
    • When you pre-register: email address, registration date and time, and selected language
    • When you visit the website: visit records and usage statistics collected through cookies (Google Analytics)

    2. How We Use It

    • Identifying members, approving beta access, and managing usage limits
    • Answering your questions
    • Improving service quality and analyzing errors
    • Preventing misuse
    • Notifying pre-registered users about the launch and beta opening
    • Analyzing website traffic

    3. Retention and Deletion

    • Account information: deleted without delay when you close your account (request deletion).
    • Questions and access records (logs): kept for 180 days from collection and then deleted automatically.
    • Pre-registration email: deleted without delay once the launch notification purpose is fulfilled or when you opt out (request deletion).
    • Website usage statistics: deleted automatically after the Google Analytics data retention period (2 months).
    • Electronic files are deleted in a way that makes them unrecoverable.

    4. Processors and International Transfers

    We entrust processing to the companies below to operate the Service, and some processing takes place outside Korea.

    Processor Task Country Data transferred
    Supabase Inc. Member authentication Japan Email, password (encrypted)
    Amazon Web Services Servers and data storage Republic of Korea (Seoul region) All items
    Google LLC and other AI API providers Analyzing questions and generating answers United States and others Questions you enter
    Google LLC (Google Analytics) Website usage analytics United States Cookie identifiers, visit records, IP address

    International transfers occur over the network when you use the Service, and the retention periods are as described in Section 3. If you do not want your information transferred abroad, you may stop using the Service and close your account.

    5. Your Rights

    You may at any time request access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information by contacting us at the address below, and the Operator will act on your request without delay.

    6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

    We store authentication information in your browser storage to keep you signed in. We also use Google Analytics to analyze website usage, which stores analytics cookies. We do not use advertising cookies.

    You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of analytics collection with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

    7. Children's Information

    Children under 14 may not sign up for the Service.

    8. Security Measures

    We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypted password storage, least-privilege access, and separate storage of service data and member information.

    10. Effective Date

    This policy takes effect on October 3, 2026. We will announce any changes through the Service.

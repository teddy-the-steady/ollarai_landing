Usage limits

  • Free accounts can ask up to 100 questions a day; the count resets at midnight Korea time. With a paid subscription, quick answers have no daily limit either.
  • Precise-answer usage resets on the 1st of each month at midnight Korea time. After using it up, you can keep asking with quick answers.
  • Paid tables show the first 100 rows, and you can load the full result from there. Very large results may take a while to load.

Billing and cancellation

  • Your subscription runs in one-month periods from your first payment and renews automatically on the same day each month until you cancel (Terms, Article 9).
  • Payments in USD are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record, which adds any applicable taxes at checkout. Customers in Korea pay ₩9,900 per month (VAT included) through Toss Payments (Article 8).
  • You can cancel anytime from the subscription settings and keep paid features until the end of the period you have paid for (Article 11).
  • You can withdraw within 7 days of a payment. If you used precise answers (the paid mode in the Terms) during that period, withdrawal is limited (Article 12).
